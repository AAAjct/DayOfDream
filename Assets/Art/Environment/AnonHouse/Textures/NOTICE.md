# Anon House texture sources

The following Poly Haven textures are released under CC0.

- Floor — [Wood Planks Dirt](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks_dirt)
- Timber — [Weathered Brown Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_brown_planks)
- Plaster — [Plastered Wall 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_02), with additional moisture staining
- Cellar — [Mossy Brick](https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_brick)
- Iron — [Corrugated Iron 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/corrugated_iron_02)
- Rug — [Dirty Carpet](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirty_carpet)

Unity uses 2K color and normal maps. The packed maps store metallic in red, ambient occlusion in green, and smoothness in alpha.
